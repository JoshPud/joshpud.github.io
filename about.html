The Weaver

The Weaver is a savage shapeshifter, fluidly transforming between the towering form of a campaign orchestrator or the viscerally funny form of a content creator, fighting proudly alongside all manner of agency creatures. They can best any foe with their imbued weapons (brain and laptop), able to adapt into whatever form is necessary to ensure victory.

Origin

Born in the frostbitten north, in the old keep of Halifax. A soldier of fortune by trade, they spent twelve long years stationed in the southern enemy's court — the sprawling, unforgiving city of London — before finally returning home in 2022. Not all the way back, mind. They settled instead in the stronghold of Leeds, close enough to remember where they came from. But posh enough to get an almond croissant on the weekend. Even now, mercenary contracts being what they are, they're summoned back into enemy territory most weeks.

Fourteen winters deep into this campaign now — hardened, but never weary — the same fire still burns that lit the first torch. At We Are Social, they lead a curious warband of designers, writers and art directors into campaigns spanning social, above the line, below the line, and the murky territory in between.
