ABOUT
✶ About

Hi, I'm
Joshua Crossley

I have walked in two centuries.

For one and a half decades I have been a storyteller, conjuring ideas for brands the whole world knows, and finding magic in places most people forget to look.

I believe stories can arrive in a thousand different forms.

A campaign. A character. An unhinged fifteen second clip that burrows into your brain and refuses to leave.

The vessel matters less than the magic inside it.

I'm still finding new ways to tell them.

I suspect I always will.

✶ Services

What can I
do for you?

I build creative platforms and brand identities for global brands. The big foundational ideas that shape how a brand shows up and what it stands for. The kind of work that gives a brand something worth saying for years, not just a campaign.

Visual storytelling across campaigns, shoots and content. I art direct with an instinct for the unexpected, finding the image, the mood and the moment that makes people stop scrolling and actually feel something.

Original content series, recurring characters and creative franchises built for brands. I concept, write, design and edit, building worlds that grow audiences and keep them coming back for more.

End-to-end creative leadership across campaigns and projects. Whether I'm bringing the vision or helping a team realise theirs, I work from brief to final frame to make sure the idea that walked into the room is still alive, and better, when it walks out.
